How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve changing your pipeline.

01

Connect a repository

A GitHub App with read access to your workflows. No runner changes, no secrets shared, nothing installed in your pipeline.

02

It builds the dependency graph

Triggers, jobs, reusable workflows, secrets, roles and the edges between them — the structure that turns an isolated line of YAML into a credential path.

03

It decides, and shows you why

Every finding carries the chain that produced it: what it observed, what it cross-referenced, what it ruled out, and the confidence it reached.
